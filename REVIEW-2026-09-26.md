# Adversarial review of `full-stack-agentic-engineer`

*Reviewed at commit `3be2bb0` (main) on 2026-09-26. This file is untracked: keep, move or delete it as you like.*

## How this was done

- Eleven independent reviewers, each in a fresh virtualenv, at tier T0 exactly as a learner on a laptop would work: no GPU, no torch, no Docker, no API keys. Nine reviewed content (one per layer; 04 and 07 split in two), one built the site and photographed it with Playwright, one ran a mechanical runnability sweep of all 32 lab directories. A separate pass covered the root documents, structure, naming and duplication.
- Every test suite and every notebook was executed (solutions must pass; blanks must stop at the first exercise). Every worked number in the layer 01–05 primers was recomputed with the cores. Upstream sources were checked where the sandbox could reach them: vLLM `main` and the 0.30.0 wheel, kube-controller-manager and kube-scheduler `release-1.34`, Kueue, the NVIDIA device plugin, PyPI.
- **CONFIRMED** means reproduced (a command, an executed cell, or a quoted line). **SUSPECTED** means plausible but not reproduced. Nothing in the repository was modified; the tree is clean.
- Reviewer notes, logs and 31 screenshots are in the session scratchpad under `review/` (paths at the end).

## Verdict

Two repositories live under one README. **Layers 01–05, plus the vLLM-internals and FlashAttention deep dives, are excellent**: every worked number reproduces from the core and is pinned by a test, upstream facts hold (about 35 vLLM claims checked against `main`, the HPA port against kube-controller-manager, Kueue and scheduler behaviour against source), simulated versus measured is labelled honestly, and all 51 core and lab notebooks run at T0 and stop where they should. **The older material (00, 06, 07 and the kernel notebooks in 04) was reorganised but never brought to that bar**: it has dead T0 paths, checks that pass empty answers, real technical errors, copy-forked "Mistral variants", office files with client data, and interview and vendor residue. **The front door undersells the good half and overclaims for the rest**: the curriculum's gap table is stale, the root README promises a shape only 01–05 have, three layer READMEs have no start-here path, and the site's sidebar is a directory listing. **Maintenance hygiene is the third theme**: there is no CI, most notebook rebuilds dirty the tree or strip the Colab cell, and counts drift.

The fixes are mostly small and local. The three larger jobs are consolidating the 07 durable sub-tree, folding the Mistral variants into adapters, and building the two topics the repo's own title implies but does not have: an LLM gateway (06) and agent memory (07).

## 3. Technical errors and contradictions

**High**

- Scale-up versus scale-out ratio. `01/gpu-deployment/gpu-deployment-exercises.md:203,230` computes "1,800 ÷ 100 = 18×" (NVLink's bidirectional total over a NIC's per-direction figure); `00/gpu-capacity-planning/PRIMER.md:110` repeats "~18× slower"; `01/gpu-deployment/gpu-deployment-primer.md:18` says "two orders of magnitude" while its own table (lines 163–168) gives 900 vs 50–100 GB/s. The roofline primer does it right (450 vs 50 per direction, about 9×) and its §1 table warns about exactly this trap. CONFIRMED.
- `00/model-landscape/mistral-primer-exercises.md:95` sizes Mistral Small 4 as "a 119B MoE with a floor of 4× HGX H100"; `open-weight-llms-primer.md:141` says it is "one ~24B model". The exercises are the primer's companion. CONFIRMED.

No test pins it. CONFIRMED by calling `exchange()`.
